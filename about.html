About Earls Colne Farm

Earls Colne Farm is a small farm in North Essex specialising in willow and other perennial crops. We have supplied willow cuttings, whips and living structures for over a decade.

Our hybrid willows are selected for fast growth and good disease resistance, making them ideal for living domes, tunnels, fedges, windbreaks and small‑scale biomass plantings.

This static site is a lightweight replacement for our previous Shopify store, designed so we only pay a fee when a customer actually checks out via Stripe.
