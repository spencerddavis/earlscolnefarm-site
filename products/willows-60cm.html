About these willow cuttings

Packs of 50 hybrid willow cuttings, each approximately 60cm long. These are ideal for:

  • Living willow screening
  • Small fedges (living fences)
  • Windbreaks and privacy lines
  • School and garden projects

Planting instructions

Plant each cutting around 15–20cm deep into moist soil, leaving at least 2 buds above the surface. Keep well-watered for the first season and remove competing weeds around the base.
